:::note[What values do the indicators in the table display]

Gauge indicators (thermometers) display a stat's relative value compared to all other characters or weapons in the table.
For example, if the weakest Predator's Second Wind HP is 1,000 and the strongest Predator's Second Wind HP is 1,500:
  * <progress value="0" max="100" style={{ width: '60px' }}>0%</progress> The weakest Predator (1,000 HP)
  * <progress value="50" max="100" style={{ width: '60px' }}>50%</progress> The average Predator (1,250 HP)
  * <progress value="100" max="100" style={{ width: '60px' }}>100%</progress> The strongest Predator (1,500 HP)
:::
