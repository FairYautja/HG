# Damage Model

The damage model is more complex than it first appears, largely due to the game's
asymmetric nature. The same attack can deal very different amounts of damage
depending on whether the target is the Predator, the Fireteam, or an NPC.

For example, a Frag Grenade deals 300 splash damage — enough to instantly down
even the most durable Fireteam class via friendly fire — yet it takes four
consecutive blasts to push the weakest Predator (1,000 HP) into Second Wind.

## Damage curves

Fireteam weapon damage falls off with distance. The game models this using Unreal Engine
[Keys and Curves](https://docs.unrealengine.com/4.27/en-US/ProgrammingAndScripting/Blueprints/UserGuide/Timelines/KeysAndCurves/),
which are piecewise linear interpolations between keyframe values (similar in concept to Bézier curves).
