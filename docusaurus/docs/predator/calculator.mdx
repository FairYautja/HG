# Predator Damage Calculator

Pick a Predator class, specialization and perks, then a Fireteam target, to see how much damage every
Predator weapon deals. Hover a damage value for the full breakdown of modifiers.

<DamageCalculator side="predator" />

## How it's calculated

```
damage = base × class melee modifier (melee only) × perks × specialization
              × target armor × hit zone × weapon headshot bonus (head only) × target perks
```

- All modifiers multiply each other. Armor multiplies incoming damage, see [Damage Model](../nerds/damage.mdx).
- Conditional perks and specializations (e.g. *from behind*, *while wounded*) are assumed to be active.
  Perks and specializations that don't change damage are left out.
- Hit zone modifiers are not applied to explosions and splash damage.
- The game files don't contain attack speeds of Predator weapons, so there is no DPS.
- Combo damage is the sum of all hits; *Hits to down* cycles through the combo.
- The Yautja Bow uses a fully charged shot. Plasma casters list the direct hit and splash separately.
