# Predator Loadout Generator

Can't decide what to play? Roll a random Predator loadout: class, specialization, two weapons,
perks and gear, always within the class's point budgets.

<LoadoutGenerator side="predator" />

## Rules

- **Weapons:** two different weapons for the primary and secondary slots. *Wrist Blades* and the
  shoulder *Plasma Caster* are never rolled, because every Predator always carries them. The
  *Hand Held Plasma Caster* is a separate weapon and can be rolled. The *Wrist Launcher* counts as gear.
- **Specialization:** a random pick from the specializations the class can use.
- **Perks and gear:** up to 3 of each. Their points never exceed the class's perk and gear budgets.
  If the budget only fits one or two items, you get one or two. A random roll can also leave
  points unused, or give no perks or gear at all.
- **Maximise points:** perks and gear are each picked from the combinations that use the most
  points the budget allows.
- **Include DLC classes:** turn it off to roll only the base-game classes (Scout, Hunter,
  Berserker and Jungle Hunter).
- **Copy link:** the link contains the roll's seed and settings, so it opens the same loadout
  for whoever you send it to.
