## Notes

:::info[Second Wind bleed]
  All Predators bleed 5 HP per second when they are in Second Wind. Theoretically, this gives them
  between 3:20 and 5:00 minutes to heal themselves. However, they take additional damage if
  the Fireteam or NPCs shoot at them.
:::
