# Introduction

There are two game modes, seven maps, and two playable factions in this game.

## Playable Characters

- [Predator](/predator) (or *Yautja* in their own language) — approximately 1,300 HP
- [Fireteam](/fireteam) member (elite human soldier) — approximately 150 HP

## Game Modes

- [Hunt](#hunt) — 1 Predator vs. 4 Fireteam members
- [Clash](#clash) — Capture the Flag

### Hunt

**1 Predator vs. 4 Fireteam members.** You can choose which side you want to play, or let the
game decide for you. Note that the random chance of being placed as the Predator is roughly
1-in-10 or lower.

#### Lobby

- You can select from your predefined character loadouts in the lobby.
- Fireteam slots are initially filled with bots and should gradually fill with real players.
- If a player leaves the lobby, their slot may be filled by someone else from the queue.
- The game waits up to 2 minutes for the lobby to fill.
- Occasionally, lobbies may be bugged and fail to start correctly.

#### Game

- Match duration is limited to 15 minutes.
- Friendly fire is disabled in public matches, except for explosive damage.
- The Predator spawns 5 seconds before the Fireteam, usually at the opposite edge of the map.
- The Fireteam receives various objectives across the map, directed by a commander called "Zeus" over the radio.
- The Predator must locate and eliminate the Fireteam before time runs out.
- NPCs shoot at both the Fireteam and the Predator, but damage dealt to the Predator is negligible.
- Fireteam members can heal using a **syrette** (fast, usable mid-combat) or a **medkit** (slow).
- Fireteam members can be **downed** by NPCs, the Predator, or explosives.
  - A downed player can be revived by another Fireteam member (real player or bot), or by using the [Self-Revive perk](/fireteam#perks).
  - Downed players bleed out and die after roughly one minute if not revived.
  - A downed player can be **claimed** (executed) by the Predator.
- The Predator can heal using a syrette or by **eating a boar**.
- The Fireteam (or, rarely, NPCs) can **Second Wind** the Predator.
  - A Second-Winded Predator cannot fight back and must flee to heal.
  - After recovering from Second Wind, roughly half of the Predator's health is restored; additional healing is recommended.
- If the Predator kills at least one Fireteam member, the remaining team is given a **second chance** at the 10-minute mark.
  - A **Reinforcement Radio** appears at one of several random locations; surviving Fireteam members can use it to revive their fallen teammates.
  - If the Predator kills the last Fireteam member while the team is waiting for reinforcements, the revived players will not return and the Predator wins.
- If the Predator fails to eliminate all Fireteam members before the last mission is completed, the game designates an **exfil point**.
  - Fireteam members must reach the exfil point and wait for a helicopter to extract them.
  - NPCs spawn at the exfil point and can hinder both factions.
  - Fireteam members can clip onto the helicopter's rope and escape. A full-health player usually cannot be killed by the Predator before the extraction completes.

#### Win Conditions

**Fireteam wins if:**
- They kill the Predator.
  - They defuse the self-destruct, **or**
  - at least one Fireteam member escapes the blast radius, **or**
  - the Predator never activates self-destruct at all.
- At least one Fireteam member exfils into the helicopter (even if the blast radius covers the exfil point, a player already on the helicopter is safe).
- The match timer expires and the Predator has not killed a single Fireteam member.

**Predator wins if:**
- All Fireteam members are eliminated.
- The match timer expires (regardless of how many Fireteam members are still alive).

### Clash

**Capture the Flag** — two teams of 4 players each compete to collect points. The match begins
with all players as Fireteam members; once a team accumulates enough points, one member is
offered the chance to become the Predator.

#### Lobby

- Teams are shown as red and blue, but the colors are relative to your perspective — your team is always blue and the enemy team is always red.
- You can change your Fireteam loadout in the lobby. Keep in mind that some [perks](/fireteam/perks) are useless or have limited effectiveness in Clash.

#### Game

- The match ends when one team reaches **500 points**.
- Friendly fire is disabled in public matches, except for explosive damage.
- Players respawn after being killed. There is no Second Wind for the Predator and no Reinforcement Radio.
- When a team accumulates enough points, the team member with the most points is offered the chance to become the Predator. The offer can be accepted or passed to another teammate.
- Both teams can field one Predator simultaneously. After a Predator is killed, the team must earn the same number of points again to unlock the next Predator offer.
