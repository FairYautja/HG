# Fireteam Damage Calculator

Pick a Fireteam class, specialization and perks, then a Predator target, to see how much damage every
Fireteam weapon deals. Hover a damage value for the full breakdown of modifiers.

<DamageCalculator side="fireteam" />

## How it's calculated

```
damage = base × class melee modifier (melee only) × perks × specialization
              × target armor × hit zone × weapon headshot bonus (head only) × target perks
```

- All modifiers multiply each other. Armor multiplies incoming damage, see [Damage Model](../nerds/damage.mdx).
- Conditional perks and specializations (e.g. *from behind*, *while wounded*) are assumed to be active.
  Perks and specializations that don't change damage are left out.
- Hit zone modifiers are not applied to explosions and splash damage.
- Damage falls off with the distance set by the slider; shotguns assume all pellets hit.
- Grenades count a direct hit plus full splash damage.
- *DPS* and *Time to down* use the fire rate and ignore reloads, misses and recoil. ↻ marks weapons
  that need more than one magazine.
