# Fireteam Loadout Generator

Can't decide what to play? Roll a random Fireteam loadout: class, specialization, primary and
secondary weapon, perks and gear, always within the class's point budgets.

<LoadoutGenerator side="fireteam" />

## Rules

- **Primary weapon:** assault rifles, sniper rifles, the CS-12 shotgun and the heavy weapons
  (D34-D, RP-103, B34S-T).
- **Secondary weapon:** pistols, SMGs, the DJL-33 and XDB-12 shotguns, the S-R3D and the PDL.
- The *Basic Knife* is always carried, so it's never rolled. The *Frag Grenade* counts as gear.
- **Specialization:** a random pick from the specializations the class can use.
- **Perks and gear:** up to 3 of each. Their points never exceed the class's perk and gear budgets.
  If the budget only fits one or two items, you get one or two. A random roll can also leave
  points unused, or give no perks or gear at all.
- **Maximise points:** perks and gear are each picked from the combinations that use the most
  points the budget allows.
- **Include DLC classes:** turn it off to roll only the base-game classes (Recon, Scout, Assault
  and Support).
- **Copy link:** the link contains the roll's seed and settings, so it opens the same loadout
  for whoever you send it to.
