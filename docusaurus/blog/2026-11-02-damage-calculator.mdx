Hello again, hunters and prey! After the last update put all the armor and body part numbers in
one place, the obvious next question was "so how many hits does it actually take?" Now the guide
can answer that for you.

{/* truncate */}

## Damage calculator

Both [Fireteam](/fireteam/calculator) and [Predator](/predator/calculator) have a new
*Damage Calculator* page. Pick your class, specialization and perks, then pick a target, where
you hit it and its defensive perks. You get a table of every weapon and attack with:

- damage per hit (or the whole combo) after all modifiers,
- hits to down the target (or to push a Predator into Second Wind),
- DPS and time to down for Fireteam guns, with a distance slider for damage falloff.

Hover any damage value to see the full breakdown: base damage, class melee modifier, perks,
specialization, armor, body part and headshot bonus. Conditional bonuses like Assassin (*from
behind*) or Fanatic (*while wounded*) are assumed to be active.

The calculator matches my in-game tests: a Berserker's Wrist Blades deal 33 HP to a Recon, and
four D34-D grenades put a Scout Predator into Second Wind.

A fun one to try: Assassin with Down Range gives Predators ×1.32 ranged damage, enough for a
thrown Combistick to down a Recon or Scout in one hit.

## Clickable classes

In the [Fireteam](/fireteam/specializations) and [Predator](/predator/specializations)
specialization tables, you can now click a class in the *Classes* column to see only the
specializations it can use. Click it again to clear the filter.

[GitHub project page](https://github.com/fairyautja/hg).
