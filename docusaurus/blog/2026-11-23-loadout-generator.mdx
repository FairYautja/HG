## Loadout generator

[Predator](/predator/loadout) and [Fireteam](/fireteam/loadout) both have a new *Loadout
Generator* page. One click rolls a class, a specialization it can use, a primary and secondary
weapon, and up to three perks and three gear items that fit the class's budgets.

Tick *Maximise perk and gear points* to never leave points on the table, turn off DLC classes if
you only own the base game, and use *Copy link* to send the exact same roll to your friends.

If you spot something off, please open an issue on the
[GitHub project page](https://github.com/fairyautja/hg).
